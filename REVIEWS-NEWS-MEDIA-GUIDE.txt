REVIEWS AND NEWS MEDIA UPLOAD GUIDE

Testimonials
1. Upload review image files to: assets/img/testimonials/
2. Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp
3. The homepage shows up to three images. The Testimonials page shows all images.

News videos
1. Upload video files to: assets/video/news/
2. Supported format: .mp4
3. The homepage links to the video-news section. The News & Updates page lists and plays all videos.
4. The displayed title is based on the filename. Use clear filenames such as community-health-event.mp4.

No database entry is needed. The pages read the media files directly from these folders.
